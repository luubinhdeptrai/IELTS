## Grammar Notation

The detailed formulas use these abbreviations:

* `S`: subject (the person or thing that performs or experiences the action)
* `S1`, `S2`: the subjects of the first and second clauses when they may be different
* `O`: object (the person or thing affected by the verb)
* `C`: complement (information that completes the meaning of the subject or object)
* `N/NP`: noun or noun phrase
* `Adj`: adjective
* `Adv`: adverb
* `V1`: base verb, such as `go`, `work`, or `study`
* `V-ing`: the `-ing` form, such as `going`, `working`, or `studying`
* `V3`: past participle, such as `gone`, `worked`, or `studied`
* `finite verb`: a verb phrase marked for tense or introduced by a modal, such as `works`, `worked`, `is working`, or `can work`
* `auxiliary`: a helping verb such as `be`, `do`, `have`, or a modal verb
* `lexical verb`: the main meaning-carrying verb, such as `work`, `need`, or `change`

## A. Everyday Actions and Repeated Habits

### 1. keep + V-ing

**General Structure:**
`S + keep + V-ing + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + keep/keeps + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** an action that continues or happens repeatedly in the present.
   * **Example:** `He keeps asking the same question.`

2. `S + kept + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** an action that continued or repeatedly happened in the past.
   * **Example:** `She kept working despite the noise.`

3. `S + will/can/must + keep + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** continued action after a modal or future auxiliary.
   * **Example:** `You must keep practicing every day.`

4. `Keep + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** an imperative that encourages someone to continue.
   * **Example:** `Keep trying until you succeed.`

**Meaning:** Tiếp tục hoặc lặp đi lặp lại một hành động.

**Usage:** Dùng khi một hành động vẫn tiếp diễn hoặc xảy ra nhiều lần, thường bất chấp khó khăn.

**Source:** [Lena_Story_Vietnamese_Translation.md](Lena_Story_Vietnamese_Translation.md) — “She just kept walking, kept waiting, and kept believing that one day her time would come.”

**English examples:**

* Keep practicing until it feels natural.
* He keeps checking his phone.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Hãy tiếp tục cố gắng dù kết quả đến chậm.
2. Cô ấy cứ hỏi tôi cùng một câu hỏi.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `keep to do something`
✅ `keep doing something`

### 2. want to + base verb

**General Structure:**
`S + want + to + V1 + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + want/wants + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a present wish or intention.
   * **Example:** `I want to improve my English.`

2. `S + wanted + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a wish or intention in the past.
   * **Example:** `She wanted to leave early.`

3. `S + may/might/will + want + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a possible or future wish.
   * **Example:** `You might want to check the address.`

4. `Do/Does + S + want + to + V1 + ...?`

   * **Used for:** asking about another person's wish.
   * **Example:** `Do you want to join us?`

**Meaning:** Muốn làm điều gì.

**Usage:** Một trong những cách thông dụng nhất để diễn đạt mong muốn hoặc ý định.

**Source:** [Lena_Story_Vietnamese_Translation.md](Lena_Story_Vietnamese_Translation.md) — “She wanted to study, but life was not kind to her.”

**English examples:**

* I want to improve my pronunciation.
* Do you want to join us?

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Tôi muốn nghỉ ngơi một lát.
2. Bạn có muốn thử món này không?

**Common mistake:**
❌ `want doing something`
✅ `want to do something`

### 3. try to + base verb

**General Structure:**
`S + try + to + V1 + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + try/tries + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a present effort to achieve a result.
   * **Example:** `I try to exercise before work.`

2. `S + tried + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** an effort made in the past.
   * **Example:** `He tried to open the door.`

3. `S + be + trying + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** an effort in progress.
   * **Example:** `We are trying to reduce our expenses.`

4. `S + try + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** testing a method to see whether it works.
   * **Example:** `Try restarting the computer.`

**Meaning:** Cố gắng làm điều gì.

**Usage:** Dùng khi ai đó nỗ lực đạt một kết quả; khác với `try + V-ing`, nghĩa là thử một phương pháp.

**Source:** [reap_sow.md](reap_sow.md) — “For the first time, instead of finishing the work quickly, he tried to finish it correctly.”

**English examples:**

* Try to arrive before nine.
* I'm trying to save more money.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Hãy cố gắng nói chậm hơn.
2. Tôi đang cố gắng không kiểm tra điện thoại quá thường xuyên.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `try doing when you mean make an effort`
✅ `try to do when you mean make an effort`

### 4. start/begin + V-ing or to + base verb

**General Structure:**
`S + start/begin + V-ing/to + V1 + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + start(s)/begin(s) + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** the beginning of an activity, especially with emphasis on the activity itself.
   * **Example:** `The baby started crying.`

2. `S + start(s)/begin(s) + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** the point at which an action or change begins.
   * **Example:** `It began to rain.`

3. `S + started/began + V-ing/to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** an activity that began in the past.
   * **Example:** `She started working here last year.`

4. `S + be + starting/beginning + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a gradual change that is beginning now.
   * **Example:** `I am beginning to understand the problem.`

**Meaning:** Bắt đầu làm điều gì.

**Usage:** Cả `start/begin doing` và `start/begin to do` thường đều đúng; dạng `V-ing` rất phổ biến trong hội thoại.

**Source:** [reap_sow.md](reap_sow.md) — “Then they began bringing better materials from the city.”

**English examples:**

* It started raining after lunch.
* She began to feel more confident.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Tôi bắt đầu học tiếng Anh nghiêm túc vào năm ngoái.
2. Trời bắt đầu tối rồi.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `start do something`
✅ `start doing/to do something`

### 5. stop + V-ing

**General Structure:**
`S + stop + V-ing + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + stop/stops + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** ending a habitual or current activity.
   * **Example:** `He stops working at six.`

2. `S + stopped + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** ending an activity in the past.
   * **Example:** `She stopped smoking last year.`

3. `S + must/should + stop + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** advice or necessity to end an activity.
   * **Example:** `You should stop worrying about it.`

4. `S + stop + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** pausing one activity in order to do another; this has a different meaning.
   * **Example:** `We stopped to buy some water.`

**Meaning:** Dừng hẳn một hành động đang diễn ra hoặc một thói quen.

**Usage:** Dùng khi không tiếp tục hành động nữa; `stop to do` có nghĩa khác: dừng việc đang làm để làm việc khác.

**Source:** [Avoid_overthinking.md](Avoid_overthinking.md) — “I stay calm because I stopped asking the sea to obey me.”

**English examples:**

* Please stop making that noise.
* He stopped drinking coffee at night.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Cô ấy đã ngừng phàn nàn về công việc.
2. Đừng liên tục tự trách mình nữa.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `stop to smoke when you mean quit smoking`
✅ `stop smoking`

### 6. instead of + noun/V-ing

**General Structure:**
`instead of + N/NP/V-ing`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + finite verb + instead of + N/NP`

   * **Used for:** choosing one thing rather than another.
   * **Example:** `We took the train instead of the bus.`

2. `S + finite verb + instead of + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** choosing one action rather than another.
   * **Example:** `She walked instead of taking a taxi.`

3. `Instead of + V-ing + ..., S + finite verb + ...`

   * **Used for:** placing the rejected action first for emphasis; both actions normally share the same subject.
   * **Example:** `Instead of complaining, he offered a solution.`

4. `S + use/choose + N/NP + instead of + N/NP`

   * **Used for:** contrasting two noun choices.
   * **Example:** `Use email instead of paper forms.`

**Meaning:** Thay vì một điều hoặc hành động khác.

**Usage:** Dùng để nêu lựa chọn thay thế; sau `instead of` dùng danh từ, đại từ hoặc động từ thêm `-ing`.

**Source:** [reap_sow.md](reap_sow.md) — “Instead of using proper leather, they pasted an old piece on it.”

**English examples:**

* Let's walk instead of taking a taxi.
* I chose tea instead of coffee.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Hãy gọi cho cô ấy thay vì gửi tin nhắn.
2. Tôi ở nhà thay vì đi dự tiệc.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `instead of to do`
✅ `instead of doing`

### 7. used to + base verb

**General Structure:**
`S + used to + V1 + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + used to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a past habit or state that is no longer true.
   * **Example:** `I used to live near the station.`

2. `S + did not use to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a situation that was not habitual or true in the past.
   * **Example:** `She did not use to like coffee.`

3. `Did + S + use to + V1 + ...?`

   * **Used for:** asking about a past habit or state.
   * **Example:** `Did you use to play tennis?`

4. `S + never used to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** emphasizing that something did not happen habitually in the past.
   * **Example:** `We never used to eat out.`

**Meaning:** Đã từng làm hoặc từng ở trạng thái nào đó trong quá khứ nhưng nay không còn.

**Usage:** Dùng cho thói quen hoặc trạng thái quá khứ đã thay đổi.

**Source:** [reap_sow.md](reap_sow.md) — “He used to repair people's old shoes in the village market.”

**English examples:**

* I used to live near the station.
* She used to be very shy.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Tôi từng thức khuya mỗi tối.
2. Nơi này trước đây từng là một hiệu sách.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `I use to work here.`
✅ `I used to work here.`

### 8. spend + time/money + V-ing

**General Structure:**
`S + spend + time/money + V-ing/on + N/NP`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + spend/spends + amount of time + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** the present amount of time used for an activity.
   * **Example:** `He spends two hours commuting each day.`

2. `S + spent + amount of time + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** time used for an activity in the past.
   * **Example:** `I spent the evening preparing my report.`

3. `S + spend/spent + amount of money + on + N/NP`

   * **Used for:** money used to buy or support something.
   * **Example:** `The city spent millions on public transport.`

4. `S + will/can + spend + time/money + V-ing/on + N/NP`

   * **Used for:** future or possible use of time or money.
   * **Example:** `We will spend more time testing the software.`

**Meaning:** Dành thời gian hoặc tiền bạc để làm điều gì.

**Usage:** Sau lượng thời gian hoặc tiền, dùng động từ `-ing` để nói hoạt động được thực hiện.

**Source:** [reap_sow.md](reap_sow.md) — “The elder son spent most of his day sitting with friends and wasting time.”

**English examples:**

* I spent an hour fixing the problem.
* They spend too much money eating out.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Cô ấy dành cả buổi sáng để chuẩn bị bài thuyết trình.
2. Chúng tôi đã tốn nhiều tiền sửa chiếc xe cũ.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `spend two hours to study`
✅ `spend two hours studying`

### 9. ask/tell + someone + to + base verb

**General Structure:**
`S + ask/tell + O + to + V1 + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + ask/tell + O + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a request with ask or an instruction with tell.
   * **Example:** `I asked him to wait outside.`

2. `S + asked/told + O + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a request or instruction in the past.
   * **Example:** `The teacher told us to revise the essay.`

3. `S + ask/tell + O + not to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** requesting or instructing someone not to act.
   * **Example:** `She told me not to worry.`

4. `O + be + asked/told + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** a passive request or instruction when the receiver is emphasized.
   * **Example:** `Passengers were asked to remain seated.`

**Meaning:** Yêu cầu, nhờ hoặc bảo ai làm điều gì.

**Usage:** Dùng tân ngữ chỉ người trước động từ nguyên mẫu có `to`; `ask` thường lịch sự hơn `tell`.

**Source:** [reap_sow.md](reap_sow.md) — “Then he asked him to open the next page.”

**English examples:**

* I asked her to wait outside.
* The doctor told me to rest.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Hãy nhờ anh ấy kiểm tra lại số liệu.
2. Giáo viên bảo chúng tôi nộp bài vào thứ Sáu.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `ask someone do something`
✅ `ask someone to do something`

### 10. allow + someone + to + base verb

**General Structure:**
`S + allow + O + to + V1 + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + allow/allows + O + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** giving permission or making an action possible.
   * **Example:** `The app allows users to edit photos.`

2. `S + allowed + O + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** permission or possibility in the past.
   * **Example:** `They allowed us to enter early.`

3. `O + be + allowed + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** stating what someone has permission to do.
   * **Example:** `Students are allowed to use calculators.`

4. `S + allow + N/NP/V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** permitting a thing or activity when no person is named.
   * **Example:** `The library does not allow smoking.`

**Meaning:** Cho phép ai làm điều gì.

**Usage:** Cấu trúc trang trọng và trung tính, thường dùng cho quy tắc, quyền hạn hoặc sự cho phép.

**Source:** [Lena_Story_Vietnamese_Translation.md](Lena_Story_Vietnamese_Translation.md) — “She allowed her to sit inside near the back table.”

**English examples:**

* The app allows users to edit photos.
* My manager allowed me to leave early.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Thư viện cho phép sinh viên mượn năm cuốn sách.
2. Cha mẹ cô ấy không cho phép cô ấy lái xe một mình.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `allow someone do something`
✅ `allow someone to do something`

### 11. make + someone + base verb

**General Structure:**
`S + make + O + V1 + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + make/makes + O + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** causing or forcing someone to do something.
   * **Example:** `The joke makes everyone laugh.`

2. `S + made + O + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** causing or forcing an action in the past.
   * **Example:** `The delay made us miss the train.`

3. `O + be + made + to + V1 + ...`

   * **Used for:** the passive form; to is required.
   * **Example:** `Employees were made to work overtime.`

4. `S + make + O + Adj/C`

   * **Used for:** causing someone or something to enter a state.
   * **Example:** `Regular practice makes me more confident.`

**Meaning:** Khiến hoặc buộc ai làm điều gì.

**Usage:** Sau `make + object` dùng động từ nguyên mẫu không `to`; ở thể bị động dùng `be made to do`.

**Source:** [reap_sow.md](reap_sow.md) — “And one day, life makes you wear what you stitched yourself.”

**English examples:**

* The news made me smile.
* Don't make him repeat everything.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Bộ phim đó khiến tôi suy nghĩ rất nhiều.
2. Tiếng ồn buộc chúng tôi phải đóng cửa sổ.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `make someone to do something`

### 14. stop/prevent + someone/something + from + V-ing

**General Structure:**
`S + stop/prevent + O + from + V-ing + ...`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + stop/stops or prevent/prevents + O + from + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** blocking an action in the present.
   * **Example:** `Strong passwords prevent criminals from accessing accounts.`

   * **Used for:** blocking an action in the past.
   * **Example:** `Heavy rain stopped us from leaving.`

   * **Used for:** a passive form that emphasizes the blocked person or thing.

4. `S + can/may + stop/prevent + O + from + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** possible prevention after a modal.
   * **Example:** `Exercise can prevent people from developing some diseases.`

**Meaning:** Ngăn ai hoặc điều gì thực hiện một hành động.

**Usage:** Dùng `from + V-ing` sau người hoặc vật bị ngăn cản; `prevent` trang trọng hơn `stop`.

**Source:** [Avoid_overthinking.md](Avoid_overthinking.md) — “You cannot stop every thought from coming.”

**English examples:**

**Practice — translate into English:**

**Common mistake:**

## B. Feelings, Thoughts, and Reactions

### 15. be tired of + noun/V-ing

**General Structure:**
`S + be/get + tired of + N/NP/V-ing`

**Detailed Structure:**

1. `S + be + tired of + N/NP`

   * **Used for:** annoyance or boredom with a person, thing, or situation.
   * **Example:** `I am tired of these delays.`

2. `S + be + tired of + V-ing + ...`

   * **Used for:** annoyance or boredom with a repeated activity.
   * **Example:** `She is tired of waiting.`

3. `S + get/got + tired of + N/NP/V-ing`

   * **Used for:** the process of becoming bored or annoyed.
   * **Example:** `He got tired of doing the same task.`

4. `S + be + tired from/after + N/NP/V-ing`

   * **Used for:** physical or mental fatigue caused by an activity, not boredom.
   * **Example:** `We were tired after walking all day.`

**Meaning:** Chán hoặc mệt mỏi vì một điều lặp lại quá lâu.

**Usage:** Dùng để thể hiện bạn không muốn tiếp tục chịu đựng một tình huống hoặc hành động.

**Source:** [Lena_Story_Vietnamese_Translation.md](Lena_Story_Vietnamese_Translation.md) — “Share it with someone who is tired of waiting.”

**English examples:**

* I'm tired of making the same mistake.
* She's tired of the constant noise.

**Practice — translate into English:**

1. Tôi chán việc phải giải thích lại mọi thứ.
2. Họ đã quá mệt mỏi với những lần trì hoãn liên tục.

**Common mistake:**
❌ `be tired to wait`
✅ `be tired of waiting`

### 16. be afraid of + noun/V-ing; be afraid to + base verb

**General Structure:**
`S + be + afraid of + N/NP/V-ing; S + be + afraid to + V1`

**Detailed Structure:**
