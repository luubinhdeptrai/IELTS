## 1. Hãy tiếp tục cố gắng dù kết quả đến chậm.

**My answer:**
`Keep trying although the result comes late.`

**Corrected:**
`Keep trying even if the results are slow to come.`

**Status:** ⚠️ Mostly correct

**Explanation:**

* `keep + V-ing` is used correctly in `keep trying`.
* `The result comes late` is understandable but not a natural collocation here. `The results are slow to come` naturally means that progress or outcomes appear slowly.
* `Even if` is preferable because the sentence encourages continued effort under a possible difficult condition; `although` normally introduces an accepted fact.

**Target pattern:**
`keep + V-ing`
