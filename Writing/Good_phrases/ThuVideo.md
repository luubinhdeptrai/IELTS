### **Topic 1: Financial Struggles and Money Management**

  - **Meaning:** To manage to survive or live with a limited amount of money or resources.
  - **Original Sentence:** "I'm 27 and currently living alone in Hanoi trying to get by on a low income".
  - **Usage:** Used to describe a survival situation where one has just enough to meet basic needs.
  - **Example:** It is difficult for many young people to get by in expensive cities without a full-time job.
- **To make ends meet**
  - **Meaning:** To have just enough money to pay for the things that you need.
  - **Original Sentence:** "...but the income was just too low to make ends meet...".
  - **Usage:** A common idiom used specifically when talking about balancing a budget or covering basic expenses.
  - **Example:** Even with two jobs, they struggled to make ends meet at the end of every month.
- **Stretched so thin**
  - **Meaning:** Having a very limited amount of something (usually money or time) that must cover many different needs.
  - **Original Sentence:** "Their meager income was stretched so thin covering debts, school fees, and daily expenses".
  - **Usage:** Used to emphasize that resources are being used to their absolute limit.
  - **Example:** The charity's budget was stretched so thin that they couldn't afford to help any new families.
- **Scraping by**
  - **Meaning:** To barely manage to survive or succeed, often with great difficulty.
  - **Original Sentence:** "...we were barely scraping by just to afford school and give the two of us a decent life".
  - **Usage:** Similar to "getting by," but carries a stronger connotation of struggle and minimal resources.
  - **Example:** They were scraping by on savings while they looked for new work.
