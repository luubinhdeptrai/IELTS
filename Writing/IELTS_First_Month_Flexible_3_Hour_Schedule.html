IELTS · Month 1 · Flexible Mode

Flexible 3-Hour Daily Study Plan

You no longer need to study for three continuous hours. Each day is divided into a high-focus morning block and a lighter evening block, while the total still equals three hours.

3 hours per day 2 hours morning + 1 hour evening Speaking + Writing ≈ 60% Monday–Sunday
21 hoursweekly total
Speaking 6 h 15 m
Writing 6 h 15 m
Reading 5 h 15 m
Listening 3 h 15 m

How this flexible plan works

Suggested times are only examples. You may move the morning block earlier or later and move the evening block to the final hour of your day. Keep the order of difficulty whenever possible.

Morning: demanding work

Timed writing, active speaking, detailed correction, and difficult reading tasks go here.

Evening: lighter work

Listening, shadowing, vocabulary review, error-log review, and relaxed reading go here.

Daily target: exactly 180 minutes

You can pause between activities. The two blocks do not need to be connected.

Monday–Sunday schedule

Default split: 06:30–08:30 and 21:00–22:00. Adjust both windows to fit your real routine.

Flexibility rule: On a busy day, you may split the morning block into two smaller sessions, but keep demanding tasks earlier and complete the remaining lighter work near the end of the day. The daily total should still reach 180 minutes.

Month 1 objective: build consistency first, then increase difficulty gradually without sacrificing recovery.
