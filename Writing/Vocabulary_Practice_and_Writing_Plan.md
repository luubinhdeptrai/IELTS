### Easily Confused Items

| Pair / group | Key difference |
|---|---|
| **transit vs transition** | `transit` = movement/transport; `transition` = change from one state to another |
| **alleviate / ease / mitigate / remedy** | all relate to solving problems, but `remedy` can be the solution itself |
| **most vs almost** | `most` = majority; `almost` = nearly |
| **label / stereotype / accuse** | `labelled as` = called; `stereotyped as` = judged by a fixed image; `accused of` = blamed for wrongdoing |
| **eminent vs imminent** | `eminent` = famous/respected; `imminent` = about to happen |
| **oral vs dental vs dentistry** | `oral` = spoken or mouth-related; `dental` = teeth-related adjective; `dentistry` = field of dental care |
| **precede vs predate** | `precede` = come before; `predate` = exist earlier than |
| **wear and tear vs wear away** | `wear and tear` = normal damage; `wear away` = gradually erode |
| **post** meanings | online post, mail, job position, physical pole/post |
| **refuse** meanings | verb `/rɪˈfjuːz/` = reject; noun `/ˈref.juːs/` = waste |

## 3. Vocabulary Learning Principles

1. **Recall before rereading.** Try to remember the Vietnamese meaning, part of speech, collocations, and one example before opening the entry.
2. **Learn chunks, not isolated words.** Learn `a decline in`, `be accused of`, `at the forefront of`, `take medication`, `normal wear and tear`.
3. **Use the word in writing within 24 hours.** A word becomes active only when you produce it.
4. **Review mistakes separately.** If you misuse `faculty`, `most`, `almost`, or `refuse`, add it to a personal error list.
5. **Prefer natural use over forced use.** In IELTS, a simple accurate sentence is better than an advanced but unnatural one.

## 4. Best Methods to Remember Vocabulary

| Method | How to do it | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Active recall | Look at the word only, then say meaning, POS, collocations, and one sentence | Forces memory retrieval |
| Spaced repetition | Review on Day 1, 2, 4, 7, 14, 30, 60 | Prevents forgetting |
| Sentence-making | Write one IELTS sentence and one daily sentence | Builds active use |
| Collocation recall | Cover the collocation list and rewrite 2-3 collocations | Prevents unnatural combinations |
| Word-family practice | Change noun → verb → adjective → adverb when possible | Expands lexical resource |
| Topic grouping | Study words by topic, such as health or crime | Helps IELTS idea generation |
| Error correction | Rewrite wrong sentences correctly | Converts mistakes into memory |

## 15. IELTS Writing Practice

### Method 1: Problem-Solution

Purpose: practise verbs such as **alleviate, mitigate, curb, remedy, deter, undertake**.

Task:

**Many cities suffer from traffic congestion and air pollution. What problems does this cause, and what solutions can governments implement?**

Sample answer:

**Traffic congestion can reduce productivity and worsen urban air quality. To alleviate this problem, governments should invest in public transit and restrict single-use vehicles in crowded areas. These measures may mitigate pollution and improve the standard of living in major cities.**  
→ Ùn tắc giao thông có thể làm giảm năng suất và làm xấu chất lượng không khí đô thị. Để giảm vấn đề này, chính phủ nên đầu tư vào giao thông công cộng và hạn chế xe dùng cá nhân trong các khu vực đông đúc. Những biện pháp này có thể giảm ô nhiễm và cải thiện mức sống ở các thành phố lớn.

Self-check:

* Did I use `alleviate` with a problem?
* Did I use `mitigate` with a negative effect?
* Did I use natural collocations?

### Method 2: Cause-Effect

Purpose: practise nouns and verbs for explaining consequences.

Task:

**Explain how overexploitation of natural resources affects rural livelihoods.**

Sample answer:

**The overexploitation of forests can damage pastureland, reduce biodiversity, and undermine local livelihoods. When land becomes barren, farmers may struggle to irrigate their fields or maintain stable income. Consequently, rural communities may face poverty and migration.**  
→ Việc khai thác quá mức rừng có thể làm hư hại đồng cỏ chăn thả, giảm đa dạng sinh học và làm suy yếu sinh kế địa phương. Khi đất trở nên cằn cỗi, nông dân có thể gặp khó khăn trong việc tưới tiêu đồng ruộng hoặc duy trì thu nhập ổn định. Do đó, các cộng đồng nông thôn có thể đối mặt với nghèo đói và di cư.

### Method 3: Advantage-Disadvantage

Purpose: practise balanced evaluation.

Task:

**Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of eco-tourism.**

Sample answer:

**Eco-tourism can protect pristine scenery and create new livelihoods for local people. However, the downside is that too many visitors may stretch local resources and damage fragile landscapes. Therefore, tourism should be carefully managed with clear environmental rules.**  
→ Du lịch sinh thái có thể bảo vệ phong cảnh nguyên sơ và tạo sinh kế mới cho người dân địa phương. Tuy nhiên, mặt hạn chế là quá nhiều du khách có thể làm căng nguồn lực địa phương và gây hại cho cảnh quan mong manh. Vì vậy, du lịch nên được quản lý cẩn thận bằng các quy định môi trường rõ ràng.

### Method 4: Opinion Essay

Purpose: practise formal stance and support.

Task:

**Some people believe vocational education is more useful than academic education. To what extent do you agree?**

Sample answer:

**Vocational education can be highly practical because it helps students attain job-specific skills and make a living more quickly. However, academic study also has merit because it develops critical thinking and research skills. In my view, both pathways should be valued, depending on a student's goals and circumstances.**  
→ Giáo dục nghề nghiệp có thể rất thực tế vì nó giúp học sinh đạt được kỹ năng chuyên môn và kiếm sống nhanh hơn. Tuy nhiên, giáo dục học thuật cũng có giá trị vì nó phát triển tư duy phản biện và kỹ năng nghiên cứu. Theo tôi, cả hai con đường đều nên được coi trọng, tùy vào mục tiêu và hoàn cảnh của học sinh.

## 18. Error-Correction Checklist

After writing, check these points:

### Vocabulary Accuracy

* [ ] Did I use the correct meaning?
* [ ] Did I use the correct part of speech?
* [ ] Did I use a natural collocation?
* [ ] Did I use the correct preposition?
* [ ] Did I confuse a similar word?

### Grammar Around Vocabulary

* [ ] If the word is a noun, did I check countability?
* [ ] If it is singular countable, did I use `a/an/the` correctly?
* [ ] If it is uncountable, did I avoid incorrect plural `-s`?
* [ ] If it is a verb, did I check transitive/intransitive use?
* [ ] Did subject and verb agree?

### Common Vietnamese-Learner Problems

| Problem | Example mistake | Better version |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong countability | `an information` | `a piece of information` |
| Wrong word form | `education can alleviation poverty` | `education can alleviate poverty` |
| Wrong preposition | `depend of` | `depend on` |
| Forced formal word | `I undertook lunch` | `I had lunch` |
| Wrong meaning | `imminent scientist` | `eminent scientist` |
| Unnatural collocation | `strong rain caused wear and tear` | `heavy use caused wear and tear` |

## 20. Final Recommended Workflow

### Daily

1. Review 8-15 old words using active recall.
2. Learn 3-6 new words from the next block.
3. Write one sentence for each new word.
4. Correct mistakes immediately.
5. Mark the next review date.

### Weekly

1. Review two entry blocks.
2. Write one topic paragraph.
3. Take one 30-word self-test.
4. Rewrite weak sentences.

### Monthly

1. Review all six blocks: A-F.
2. Choose 50 hard words and retest them.
3. Write six IELTS-style paragraphs.
4. Write two essay plans.
5. Update the hard word list.

### The Core Rule

Do not ask only: **"Do I know this word?"**

Ask:

**"Can I use this word correctly in a sentence or paragraph without looking?"**

That is the real test of active vocabulary.
