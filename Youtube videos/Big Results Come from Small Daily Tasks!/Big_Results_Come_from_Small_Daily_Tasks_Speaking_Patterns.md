> **Source Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KwmBvCew4IU&list=LL&index=6
>
> **Source transcript:** `Big Results Come from Small Daily Tasks!.md`
>
> **Goal:** turn useful everyday sentences from the transcript into reusable speaking patterns and active speaking ability.

## How to use this file

For each pattern:

1. Listen to the original sentence in the video.
2. Shadow it 3–5 times.
3. Learn the **reusable pattern/chunk**, not only the original sentence.
4. Create at least 3 new sentences.
5. Create 1 sentence about your own life.
6. Say the sentence again without looking.

## High-value speaking patterns

### 1. I hope you are feeling ...

**Original sentence #2:**
> I'm really happy to be here with you today and I hope you are feeling good.

**Vietnamese:** Tôi thực sự rất vui khi được ở đây cùng các bạn hôm nay và tôi hy vọng các bạn đang cảm thấy khỏe khoắn.

**Reusable pattern:** **I hope + subject + be/verb ...**

**When to use it:** Bày tỏ hy vọng hoặc lời chúc một cách tự nhiên.

**More examples:**

- I hope you're feeling better today.
- I hope everything goes well.
- I hope you enjoy the rest of your day.
