# Common Daily Conversation Phrases

> Extracted from the provided English podcast transcript.  
> Focus: natural, reusable phrases for everyday conversation.

## 1. Greeting & Starting a Conversation

| Phrase | Vietnamese meaning | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hello everyone.** | Xin chào mọi người. | Greeting a group of people. | Hello everyone. How are you today? |
| **Welcome back.** | Chào mừng bạn quay trở lại. | When someone returns to a class, channel, podcast, etc. | Welcome back! It’s good to see you again. |
| **I’m so happy you’re here.** | Tôi rất vui vì bạn ở đây. | Warmly welcoming someone. | I’m so happy you’re here today. |
| **I’m so glad to be here.** | Tôi rất vui khi được ở đây. | Showing happiness about joining someone. | I’m so glad to be here with you. |
| **How is your day?** | Hôm nay của bạn thế nào? | Asking about someone’s current day. | Hi, Anna. How is your day? |
| **How was your day?** | Hôm nay của bạn thế nào? | Asking about someone’s day, usually later in the day. | You’re home! How was your day? |
| **Let me tell you about my day.** | Để tôi kể cho bạn nghe về ngày hôm nay của tôi. | Starting to share what happened during your day. | Let me tell you about my day. It was really busy. |
| **How about you?** | Còn bạn thì sao? | Returning a question to the other person. | I’m doing well. How about you? |
| **Are you ready?** | Bạn sẵn sàng chưa? | Before starting an activity or conversation. | Are you ready? Let’s go. |
| **Let’s start.** | Hãy bắt đầu thôi. | Starting an activity. | Okay, let’s start. |

## 2. Talking About How You Feel

| Phrase | Vietnamese meaning | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I’m tired but happy.** | Tôi mệt nhưng vui. | When you feel tired but satisfied. | I worked all day. I’m tired but happy. |
| **I felt a little sleepy.** | Tôi cảm thấy hơi buồn ngủ. | Describing mild sleepiness. | I felt a little sleepy this morning. |
| **I was in a good mood.** | Tôi có tâm trạng tốt. | Saying you felt positive or cheerful. | I was in a good mood after breakfast. |
| **I was not very happy and not very sad.** | Tôi không quá vui cũng không quá buồn. | Describing a neutral mood. | I was not very happy and not very sad today. |
| **I started to feel more awake.** | Tôi bắt đầu cảm thấy tỉnh táo hơn. | When sleepiness begins to disappear. | After washing my face, I started to feel more awake. |
| **I felt rested.** | Tôi cảm thấy được nghỉ ngơi đầy đủ. | After having enough sleep or rest. | I slept well, so I felt rested. |
| **I felt satisfied.** | Tôi cảm thấy hài lòng. | After completing something successfully. | I finished all my tasks and felt satisfied. |
| **I felt relieved.** | Tôi cảm thấy nhẹ nhõm. | After a stressful situation ends. | The meeting went well, so I felt relieved. |
| **I felt frustrated.** | Tôi cảm thấy bực bội. | When something is annoying or difficult. | My computer stopped working, and I felt frustrated. |
| **I felt stressed.** | Tôi cảm thấy căng thẳng. | Talking about stress. | I felt stressed before the meeting. |
| **I felt much better.** | Tôi cảm thấy tốt hơn nhiều. | After your mood or condition improves. | After the walk, I felt much better. |
| **I felt lighter and calmer.** | Tôi cảm thấy nhẹ nhõm và bình tĩnh hơn. | After relaxing or reducing stress. | After talking to my friend, I felt lighter and calmer. |
| **I feel grateful.** | Tôi cảm thấy biết ơn. | Expressing gratitude. | I feel grateful for everything that happened today. |
| **I’m okay.** | Tôi ổn. | A simple, neutral response about your condition. | Today was difficult, but I’m okay. |

## 4. Talking About Work, Study & Tasks

| Phrase | Vietnamese meaning | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I had several tasks to finish.** | Tôi có vài công việc cần hoàn thành. | Talking about work or study tasks. | I had several tasks to finish before lunch. |
| **I had an important meeting.** | Tôi có một cuộc họp quan trọng. | Talking about your schedule. | I had an important meeting this afternoon. |
| **The meeting went well.** | Cuộc họp diễn ra tốt đẹp. | Saying a meeting was successful. | Luckily, the meeting went well. |
| **We talked about a project.** | Chúng tôi nói về một dự án. | Common work/study conversation. | We talked about a new project at work. |
| **Everyone shared their ideas.** | Mọi người chia sẻ ý tưởng của mình. | Describing a meeting or discussion. | Everyone shared their ideas during the meeting. |
| **We made some good decisions.** | Chúng tôi đã đưa ra một số quyết định tốt. | After a productive discussion. | We talked for an hour and made some good decisions. |
| **I finished my tasks.** | Tôi đã hoàn thành công việc của mình. | Saying your work is complete. | I finished my tasks before 5 p.m. |
| **I was in the middle of something important.** | Tôi đang làm dở một việc quan trọng. | When something interrupts you. | My phone rang while I was in the middle of something important. |

| **I got through it.** | Tôi đã vượt qua/xử lý được nó. | After finishing something difficult or stressful. | It was a hard day, but I got through it. |

## 7. Unexpected Events & Problems

| Phrase | Vietnamese meaning | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Did anything unexpected happen?** | Có chuyện gì bất ngờ xảy ra không? | Asking about unexpected events. | Did anything unexpected happen today? |
| **Something unexpected happened.** | Có chuyện bất ngờ xảy ra. | Starting a story about an unexpected event. | Something unexpected happened at work today. |
| **My computer stopped working.** | Máy tính của tôi ngừng hoạt động. | Talking about a technology problem. | My computer stopped working during the meeting. |
| **I tried to stay calm.** | Tôi cố giữ bình tĩnh. | During a stressful situation. | I tried to stay calm and solve the problem. |
| **It was a small problem.** | Đó chỉ là một vấn đề nhỏ. | Describing a minor issue. | It was a small problem, so don’t worry. |
| **It was not a big problem.** | Đó không phải vấn đề lớn. | Reassuring someone or reflecting on an issue. | Don’t worry. It was not a big problem. |
| **Looking back now, ...** | Bây giờ nhìn lại thì... | Reflecting on a past event. | Looking back now, I worried too much. |
| **In that moment, ...** | Vào lúc đó... | Describing how you felt at a specific time. | In that moment, I felt really nervous. |
| **You won’t believe what happened today!** | Bạn sẽ không tin chuyện gì đã xảy ra hôm nay đâu! | Starting an exciting or surprising story. | You won’t believe what happened today! I met my old teacher. |

## 8. Describing Your Day Naturally

| Phrase | Vietnamese meaning | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **My day was really good.** | Ngày hôm nay của tôi rất tốt. | Simple positive answer to “How was your day?” | My day was really good, thank you. |
| **I had a relaxing and peaceful day.** | Tôi đã có một ngày thư giãn và yên bình. | Describing a calm day. | I stayed home and had a relaxing and peaceful day. |
| **It was a good day.** | Đó là một ngày tốt đẹp. | Simple general description. | It was a good day overall. |
| **Nothing too exciting.** | Không có gì quá đặc biệt/thú vị. | Saying the day was ordinary. | Nothing too exciting happened today. |
| **I had such a busy day today.** | Hôm nay tôi đã có một ngày rất bận rộn. | Emphasizing a busy day. | I had such a busy day today. |
| **I’m exhausted.** | Tôi kiệt sức/rất mệt. | Stronger than “I’m tired.” | I worked for ten hours. I’m exhausted. |
| **Today was really hectic.** | Hôm nay thực sự rất bận rộn và căng thẳng. | Describing a very busy, somewhat stressful day. | Today was really hectic at the office. |
| **I had so many things to do.** | Tôi có quá nhiều việc phải làm. | Describing a busy schedule. | I had so many things to do this morning. |
| **Today was a tough day.** | Hôm nay là một ngày khó khăn. | Describing a difficult day. | Honestly, today was a tough day. |
| **I felt a little overwhelmed.** | Tôi cảm thấy hơi quá tải. | When there is too much to handle. | I had too much work and felt a little overwhelmed. |
| **Today was not my best day.** | Hôm nay không phải là ngày tốt nhất của tôi. | A gentle way to say the day was bad. | Today was not my best day, but tomorrow will be better. |
| **I’m looking forward to tomorrow.** | Tôi mong chờ ngày mai. | Expressing positive anticipation. | Today was difficult, but I’m looking forward to tomorrow. |
| **It was an ordinary day.** | Đó là một ngày bình thường. | Describing a normal day. | It was an ordinary day, nothing special. |
| **Nothing special.** | Không có gì đặc biệt. | Very common short response. | How was work? — Nothing special. |
| **Everything is fine.** | Mọi thứ đều ổn. | Reassuring someone. | Don’t worry. Everything is fine. |
| **Just a quiet day at home.** | Chỉ là một ngày yên tĩnh ở nhà. | Briefly describing a calm day. | What did you do today? — Just a quiet day at home. |
| **Today was a productive day.** | Hôm nay là một ngày hiệu quả. | When you completed useful work. | I finished a lot of work, so today was a productive day. |
| **I feel tired but satisfied.** | Tôi cảm thấy mệt nhưng hài lòng. | After a productive day. | I finished everything. I feel tired but satisfied. |

## 10. Useful Conversation Reactions

| Phrase | Vietnamese meaning | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **That sounds wonderful.** | Nghe thật tuyệt. | Reacting positively to someone’s story. | I had dinner with my family. — That sounds wonderful. |
| **That sounds lovely.** | Nghe thật dễ chịu/tuyệt. | Warm positive reaction. | We had tea in the garden. — That sounds lovely. |
| **That sounds delicious.** | Nghe ngon quá. | Reacting to food. | I made homemade soup. — That sounds delicious. |
| **That is a great habit.** | Đó là một thói quen tốt. | Praising someone’s routine. | You exercise every morning? That is a great habit. |
| **I completely understand.** | Tôi hoàn toàn hiểu. | Showing empathy or agreement. | I completely understand how you feel. |
| **I completely agree.** | Tôi hoàn toàn đồng ý. | Strong agreement. | I completely agree with you. |
| **That is very true.** | Điều đó rất đúng. | Agreeing with a statement. | Sleep is important. — That is very true. |
| **Exactly.** | Chính xác./Đúng vậy. | Strong, short agreement. | Exactly. That’s what I mean. |
| **Absolutely.** | Hoàn toàn đúng./Chắc chắn rồi. | Strong agreement or confirmation. | Absolutely. I’d love to come. |
| **Oh really?** | Ồ, thật à? | Showing interest or surprise. | I met John yesterday. — Oh really? |
| **I know that feeling.** | Tôi hiểu cảm giác đó. | Showing empathy based on similar experience. | I was so nervous before the exam. — I know that feeling. |
| **That’s an important lesson.** | Đó là một bài học quan trọng. | Reflecting on an experience. | We should always back up our files. That’s an important lesson. |

## 13. Mini Practice Dialogue

```text
A: How was your day?

B: I had such a busy day today. I had so many things to do.

A: Oh really? What happened?

B: I had an important meeting in the afternoon. Luckily, the meeting went well.

A: That sounds good. How did you feel afterward?

B: I felt relieved. I was tired, but satisfied.

A: I know that feeling. What did you do after work?

B: I went for a short walk and then made dinner.

A: That sounds lovely.

B: Yeah, I felt much better after the walk. How about you?
```

## Learning Tip

Do not try to memorize every phrase at once.

A practical method:

1. Learn **5 phrases per day**.
2. Say each phrase aloud several times.
3. Create **one sentence about your real life** with each phrase.
4. Use the phrases when answering common questions such as:
   - *How was your day?*
   - *What did you do today?*
   - *How did you feel?*
5. Review yesterday’s phrases before learning new ones.
