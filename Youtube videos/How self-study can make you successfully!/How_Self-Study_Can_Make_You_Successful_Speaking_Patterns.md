# Speaking Patterns — How Self-Study Can Make You Successful

> Source: `How Self-Study Can Make You Successful.md`
>
> Goal: turn reusable chunks from the transcript into active spoken English.

## How to study each pattern

1. Listen to the original sentence in the video.
2. Shadow it 3–5 times.
3. Memorize the **pattern**, not the entire sentence.
4. Create 3 new examples.
5. Create 1 sentence about your real life.
6. Say them again without looking.

**Vietnamese:** Tôi hy vọng các bạn thích lắng nghe.

**When to use it:** Bày tỏ hy vọng/mong muốn.

**More examples:**

## Speaking workflow for this transcript

### 1. Shadow → Change → Personalize

Example:

> Original: `What do you think about self-study?`  
> Pattern: `What do you think about + noun/V-ing?`  
> New: `What do you think about working from home?`  
> Personal: `What do you think about learning English through podcasts?`

### 2. Question → Answer → Reason → Example

Pause whenever the podcast asks a question. Answer it yourself **before** listening to the original answer.

Example:

> I think self-study is useful. It gives me more freedom. For example, I can choose when and what I want to study.

### 3. Listen → Pause → Reconstruct

Listen to one sentence, pause, reproduce it from memory, then compare with the transcript. This trains retrieval instead of passive imitation.

### 4. Substitution drill

Take one pattern and create 5 rapid variations. Example with `When I ..., I ...`:

- When I feel tired, I rest.
- When I learn a new word, I write it down.
- When I make a mistake, I correct it.
- When I lose focus, I put my phone away.
- When I have free time, I listen to English.

### 5. 60-second self-talk

Speak for one minute about one topic without reading:

- My study habits
- How I stay focused
- How I learn English by myself
- Problems I have when studying
- What works for me

Try to reuse at least **5 patterns** from this file.

### 6. Retell in your own words

After each podcast section, retell it using: **Main idea → Personal opinion → Personal example**.
